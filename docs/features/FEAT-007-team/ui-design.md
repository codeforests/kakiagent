## SCR-APP-045 — Team (members, invitations, roles, rights)

- Route `/settings/team` (unchanged). Order: `h1` "Team", `page-lede` "Invite your staff and choose what each person can do." and, at the right of the title row (below it < 640), `primary` "Invite someone" (Owner, Admin). Then the **Members** section, the **Invitations** section (only when there are any), then FEAT-006's "Two-step verification" card, unchanged. The FEAT-007 placeholder goes.
- **Members** (`h2` "Members", count in text-secondary "· {n}"). Desktop `table`, columns: Person (`avatar` with the email's first letter, email, and "You" `chip` neutral on your row), Role (text: Owner / Admin / Agent; an Agent with rights shows a second line "Can approve" with Lucide `badge-check` in success text), Two-step (`status-badge` success "On" or neutral "Off"), Last active ("Today 14:02", "3 Oct", or "Never"), and an actions column with a `ghost` icon button `more-horizontal` labelled "Actions for {email}" opening a `menu`. Deactivated members sort last, the row text in text-secondary with `status-badge` neutral "Deactivated" in the Role column. Below 768 px the table becomes the stacked `list`: email (+ "You"), then "Role · Two-step on · Last active 3 Oct", the actions button at the right.
- **Row menu** items (built by `memberActions`, so a row with no actions shows no button; the Owner's row and your own never have one):
  - "Change role" → `dialog` "Change {email}'s role" with a `radio` group: Admin "Manages the knowledge base, channels, rules and the team." / Agent "Works the inbox and leads." The current role is checked; `primary` "Change role" disabled until a different one is picked; help line "They'll be signed out of {workspace} and sign back in with the new role." When an Agent with rights becomes an Admin, add "Approval rights come with the Admin role."; when anyone becomes an Agent, add "They won't be able to approve until the Owner grants it again." Success: toast "{email} is now an {Role}."
  - "Give approval rights" / "Remove approval rights" (Owner only, active Agents only) → immediate (not sensitive) with toast "{email} can now approve quotes and discounts." / "{email} can no longer approve." The menu shows it as a `toggle`-style item with a check when on.
  - "Deactivate" (danger, after a divider) → `confirm-dialog` "Deactivate {email}?" / "They'll be signed out of {workspace} straight away and can't come back until someone reactivates them. Their past replies and notes stay." / `danger` "Deactivate". Toast "{email} is deactivated."
  - "Reactivate" (deactivated rows) → `confirm-dialog` "Reactivate {email}?" / "They'll get back in as {Role}." / `primary` "Reactivate". Toast "{email} can sign in again."
- **Invitations** (`h2` "Invitations"): rows of email, role, "Invited by {email} · expires 7 Nov" (pending) or `status-badge` warning "Expired" (expired), and `status-badge` neutral "Sending…" while `delivery` is `queued` with help "We'll keep trying to send it." Actions (buttons, not a menu, because there are only two): `secondary` "Resend" (expired or pending; toast "New invitation sent to {email}. The old link no longer works.") and `ghost` "Revoke" → `confirm-dialog` "Revoke the invitation to {email}?" / "The link stops working." / `danger` "Revoke". Toast "Invitation revoked."
- **Invite dialog** (`dialog`, form): `h2` "Invite someone". Field "Email address" (`text-input` type email, autocomplete off). `radio` group "Role" with the two descriptions above, Agent checked by default. Note "The invitation link works once and for 7 days, only for this email address." `secondary` "Cancel", `primary` "Send invitation" ("Sending…" while pending). Success: dialog closes, the new row appears at the top of Invitations, toast "Invitation sent to {email}." Errors inline in an `alert` danger at the top of the dialog, field keeps focus: `already_member` "{email} is already in your team."; `member_deactivated` "{email} was deactivated." with link "Reactivate them" (opens that row's reactivate dialog); `invite_pending` "There's already an invitation for {email}." with link "Resend it"; `rate_limited` "You've sent a lot of invitations. Try again in an hour."; invalid email "Enter an email address like name@business.sg" under the field.
- **States:** loading (`skeleton` table, 3 rows), error (`error-state` "We couldn't load your team." [Try again]), only the Owner (Members has one row, plus an `empty-state` in place of Invitations: "Just you so far. Invite your staff so they can take over chats and work the inbox." [Invite someone]), pending and expired invitations, queued delivery, deactivated members, saving (the dialog's button busy), a failed change (`alert` danger in the dialog: "That didn't save. Try again."), and **read-only for Agents**: no Members or Invitations sections (the API answers 403) but an `alert` info "Only the Owner and Admins can manage the team." above the two-step card.
- Focus returns to the row's actions button (or "Invite someone") when a dialog closes; after a row disappears (revoke) focus moves to the Invitations heading.

## SCR-APP-004 — Accept invitation

- Route `/invite`, in the auth layout (`pages/auth-layout.tsx`: centred card, logo, no app shell), reachable signed in or not. The page reads `#token=` once, removes it from the address bar (`history.replaceState`) and posts it to lookup; a page without a token is the **invalid** state.
- **Valid, signed in as the invited address:** `h1` "Join {tenantName}", body "{invitedBy} invited you to join as {an Admin | an Agent}." `primary` full-width "Join {tenantName}" ("Joining…" while pending). Success → `/` in the joined workspace with toast "Welcome to {tenantName}."
- **Valid, not signed in:** `h1` "Join {tenantName}", the same body, then "Sign in as **{email}** to join." `primary` "Email me a sign-in link" (sends the link to that address only; then the card says "Check your inbox. The link signs you in and joins {tenantName}." with `ghost` "Send again", as SCR-APP-001's sent state) and, when Google is configured, `secondary` "Continue with Google" (stores the token in `sessionStorage`, goes to `/api/v1/auth/google/start?next=invite`; on return the page sees the stored token and joins without another click, showing "Joining…").
- **Wrong email** (signed in as someone else, or Google returned another address): `alert` warning title "This invitation is for {email}", body "You're signed in as {signedInAs}. Sign out, then sign in as {email} to join." `primary` "Sign out and continue" (signs out this browser only, then shows the not-signed-in state). `ghost` link "Go to my dashboard" (signed in only).
- **Expired:** `h1` "This invitation has expired", body "Invitations last 7 days. Ask {invitedBy} to send a new one." No action besides `ghost` "Go to sign in" (or "Go to my dashboard" when signed in).
- **Used:** `h1` "This invitation has already been used", body "If you joined already, sign in to open {tenantName}." `primary` "Sign in" / "Go to my dashboard".
- **Revoked or invalid:** `h1` "This invitation isn't valid", body "It may have been withdrawn or replaced by a newer one. Ask the person who invited you to send it again." `ghost` "Go to sign in".
- **Loading:** `skeleton` lines in the card with `aria-busy`. **Error** (network or 5xx on lookup or join): `alert` danger "Something went wrong. Try again." with `secondary` "Try again" (the token is kept in memory, never re-read from the URL).
- Document title "Join {tenantName} · KakiAgent" (valid) or "Invitation · KakiAgent". Focus on the `h1` on each state change.

## Tenant switcher (top bar, design.md `tenant-switcher`)

- `shell/tenant-switcher.tsx` replaces `topbar__tenant`. With one workspace it is the plain name, as today (`data-testid="tenant-name"` kept on the name). With two or more it is a `menu` trigger: the name + Lucide `chevrons-up-down` (16), `aria-label="Switch business, current: {name}"`.
- Menu: `menu__label` "Your businesses", then one `DropdownMenu.RadioItem` per active membership in name order: name (body) and role (caption, text-secondary), the current one checked (`aria-checked`). No deactivated or pending workspaces appear (AUTH-04).
- Choosing another: the menu closes, the trigger shows a `spinner` after 300 ms, the session switches, the query cache clears, the socket reconnects and the app goes to `/`; toast "Switched to {name}." A failure (404: no longer a member) shows toast-free `alert` in the banner slot: "You're no longer a member of {name}." and refreshes the list.
- Touch: items 44 px; the trigger truncates long names with an ellipsis (max 24 ch) and keeps the full name in the label.
