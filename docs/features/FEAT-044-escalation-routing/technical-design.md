# Technical design: FEAT-044 — Escalation routing, "talk to a person", reminders

> Implements: INB-05 (escalation routing round-robin or to a specific person; staff notified by email (Telegram FEAT-037, web push FEAT-048); reminders every N minutes, default 10, configurable, until claimed; after hours the visitor is told when staff will respond), AGT-06 (p: `escalate_to_human`, audited; the other tools arrive with their features), CHN-13 (p → complete: the widget's "talk to a person" control triggers escalation; after-hours lead capture with FEAT-049), AGT-04 (p: the offer of a person in the unsure text; lead capture with FEAT-049), AGT-09 (p: the after-hours escalation notice; lead capture with FEAT-049), INB-01 (p: the assignee filter widened to escalation assignees, FEAT-043 D7) · Realizes: UC-23 steps 1 and 2, alternate flows 2a (p: no lead capture yet) and 2b · Screens: SCR-WGT-002 (p: Talk to a person enabled, escalation notice), SCR-APP-014 (p: assignee on escalated rows, filter), SCR-APP-015 (p: escalation strip, divider), SCR-APP-045 (p: Escalation routing card, D2) (see [ui-design.md](ui-design.md)) · Module: `inbox` (escalations), `agent` (dispatcher handler), `tenancy` (business minutes), architecture §5.2, ADR-012, ADR-013, ADR-025
> Status: designed · 2026-11-01

## 1. Intent

A visitor who wants a person gets one through a deterministic path. Pressing "Talk to a person" in the widget, or asking for a person in words (triage's `escalate` task, AGT-06 `escalate_to_human`), or a reply that failed verification twice (FEAT-022 D2) moves the conversation to Escalated and creates one open escalation. The escalation is assigned to a team member by the tenant's routing setting (round-robin among active members, or always one named person), and that person is alerted through FEAT-052's `notify`. If nobody takes it over within N business minutes (default 10), the assignee is reminded, and from the third reminder the owners and admins are reminded too, every N minutes until someone takes over or closes the conversation. The visitor sees a notice: "I've asked the team to join…", or after hours when the team will be back (AGT-09). Taking over (FEAT-043) claims it; every move out of Escalated resolves the escalation and cancels its pending alerts. The AI keeps answering while the visitor waits (gate unchanged).

## 2. Codebase context

- **Lifecycle** (`inbox/lifecycle.ts`): `TRANSITIONS` already allows `ai | waiting_approval → escalated` and `escalated → ai | human | closed`; `transition` is the only writer of `status`, locks the row, writes `conversation.state_changed` `{ from, to, reason }`, and deletes the owner row on close and `human → ai` (FEAT-043). `LIFECYCLE_ACTIONS` feed `threadEvents` dividers.
- **Ownership** (`inbox/ownership.ts`, FEAT-043): `takeOver` moves `escalated → human` through `transition`; FEAT-043 §8 asks this feature to stop reminders once the conversation has an owner and to widen the assignee filter (D7).
- **Dispatcher** (`agent/dispatch.ts`): `escalate` is a `state` task with no handler today (`unhandled`); `ConversationPort` has `flag`, `recordAbuse`, `endAi`. Compose's `escalated` outcome (two failed verifications) stores the escalation text and `guardrail.escalated` but changes no state (`turn.ts` `store(`).
- **Gate** (`agent/gate.ts`): escalated conversations are still answered until a person owns them. Unchanged.
- **Notifications** (`@kaki/platform/notifications`, FEAT-052): `notify(tx, { tenantId, kind, recipients, urgent, subject, dedupeKey })` and `cancelNotifications(tx, tenantId, subject, kind?)`; kinds `escalation | approval_pending | hot_lead | test`; `app_agent_visitor` and `app_dashboard` may insert and cancel; the sender maps the email mute per kind and holds non-urgent alerts in quiet hours; `links.ts` maps `escalation` to `/inbox/{conversationId}`.
- **Members** are visible only to the identity zone (`memberships`); counting across zones uses SECURITY DEFINER functions owned by NOLOGIN roles (`tenant_mfa_enrolment()`, 0037). Routing from the visitor zone needs the same (D3).
- **Hours** (`tenancy/hours.ts` `openStatus`, `hoursOn`; `visitorTenantState` carries `hours`, `openOnPublicHolidays`, `holidays`): enough to compute business minutes. `agent/hours-text.ts` formats `next_open`.
- **Timers** (ADR-012): the scheduler calls a SECURITY DEFINER function on the outbox zone that queues a job on `conversation-turns`; the agent-worker runs it (FEAT-042 auto-close: `inbox_queue_auto_close`, `TurnJob` kind `auto_close`).
- **Widget** (FEAT-033 D2): `controls.talkToPerson` is hard-coded false in `GET /widget/v1/config`; `menuItems` shows the item only when the flag is on; `onTalkToPerson` is unset. Notices render server-side text in `system-notice` rows (FEAT-023, FEAT-043).
- **Disclosure** (FEAT-024 D2): the fixed core leaves out design.md §6's "A person can take over at any time." until takeover exists.

## 4. Schema changes (migration `0045_escalations.sql`; Drizzle DDL, then hand-written RLS, grants, checks and functions in the same file)

- **New table `escalations`**: `id uuid pk default gen_random_uuid()`, `tenant_id uuid not null`, `conversation_id uuid not null` (FK `(tenant_id, conversation_id) → conversations`), `source text not null` (check in the three sources), `urgent boolean not null`, `after_hours boolean not null`, `assignee_user_id uuid` (null only if no member could be found, which the candidates function makes impossible while the tenant has an owner), `created_at timestamptz not null default now()`, `reminders int not null default 0`, `next_reminder_at timestamptz`, `reminder_queued_at timestamptz`, `resolved_at timestamptz`, `resolution text` (check `claimed | closed | returned`; `(resolved_at is null) = (resolution is null)`), `resolved_by uuid`. Partial unique index `(tenant_id, conversation_id) where resolved_at is null` (one open escalation per conversation); index `(next_reminder_at) where resolved_at is null`; index `(tenant_id, assignee_user_id) where resolved_at is null` (assignee filter). Forced RLS with the tenant policy, name in `TENANT_SCOPED_TABLES`, a case in `rls.int.test.ts`.
- **New table `escalation_routing`** (one row per tenant, tenant configuration): `tenant_id uuid pk`, `mode text not null default 'round_robin'` (check), `person_user_id uuid`, check `mode <> 'person' or person_user_id is not null`, `reminder_minutes int not null default 10` (check 1–240), `last_assigned_user_id uuid` (round-robin cursor), `updated_at`, `updated_by uuid`. Forced RLS, policy, `TENANT_SCOPED_TABLES`, RLS case.
- **Grants**: `app_agent_visitor` SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE `(urgent, reminders, next_reminder_at, reminder_queued_at, assignee_user_id, resolved_at, resolution, resolved_by)` on `escalations`; SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE `(last_assigned_user_id)` on `escalation_routing`. `app_dashboard` SELECT, UPDATE `(resolved_at, resolution, resolved_by)` on `escalations` (staff take-over and close resolve it); SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE `(mode, person_user_id, reminder_minutes, updated_at, updated_by)` on `escalation_routing`.
- **`escalation_candidates()`** RETURNS TABLE `(user_id uuid, role text)`: active members of `app_current_tenant()`, ordered by `user_id`; SECURITY DEFINER, owned by new NOLOGIN role `kaki_escalation_route` with SELECT `(tenant_id, user_id, role, status)` on `memberships` and a `FOR SELECT TO kaki_escalation_route USING (tenant_id = app_current_tenant())` policy; EXECUTE to `app_agent_visitor` and `app_dashboard` only (D3). It returns IDs and roles, never names or emails.
- **`inbox_queue_escalation_reminders()`** RETURNS TABLE `(tenant_id uuid, queued int)`, owned by `kaki_inbox_timer` (0042) with SELECT, UPDATE `(next_reminder_at, reminder_queued_at)` on `escalations` and a policy for it: open escalations with `next_reminder_at <= now()`, `LIMIT 500 FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, sets `next_reminder_at = null, reminder_queued_at = now()`, inserts one outbox row per escalation (`topic 'conversation-turns'`, `group_key` the conversation ID, payload `{ kind: 'escalation_reminder', tenantId, escalationId }`); EXECUTE to `app_outbox`.
- **`inbox_queue_auto_close()`** replaced (`CREATE OR REPLACE`, same owner and grants): skips `escalated` conversations as well as `closed` (D5).
- **`messages`**: `NOTICE_KINDS` gains `escalated`; the notice check is rewritten with the five kinds.
- **`notifications`**: `NOTIFICATION_KINDS` gains `escalation_reminder`; its kind check is rewritten (D6).

## 5. Component design

### 5.1 Escalations (`packages/inbox/src/escalation.ts`, exported from the index)

- `requestEscalation(tx, { tenantId, conversationId, source, now, hours })` → `{ status: 'escalated' | 'already_escalated' | 'with_person', escalation, notice, conversation }`. `hours` is the tenant's `{ hours, openOnPublicHolidays, holidays } | null` (null: treated as always open). In order:
  1. Lock the conversation (`transition`'s row lock). Closed → `ConversationClosedError` (API 409 `closed`). An owner row or status `human` → `with_person`, nothing written.
  2. An open escalation exists → `already_escalated`; if it isn't urgent and the new source is a visitor's and it is business hours, set `urgent = true` and `notify` the assignee urgently (dedupe `escalation:{id}:urgent`). No second notice.
  3. Otherwise: `afterHours = !openStatus(...).open`; `urgent = source !== 'unverified' && !afterHours` (D1); `assignee = route(tx, tenantId)` (§5.2); insert the escalation with `next_reminder_at = addBusinessMinutes(now, reminderMinutes, hours)` (§5.3); `transition(… to 'escalated', actor: visitor (button, asked) or agent (unverified))`; audit `escalation.assigned` `{ escalationId, source, urgent, afterHours }` with `subject_refs` the assignee's ID (ADR-014: IDs and codes); `notify(tx, { kind: 'escalation', recipients: [assignee], urgent, subject: { type: 'conversation', id }, dedupeKey: 'escalation:{id}' })`.
  4. The notice (not for `unverified`, whose reply text already says the team will confirm): one `system` message, `notice_kind 'escalated'`, in the conversation's language (FEAT-043 `staffNotice`'s rule). Business hours: en "I've asked the team to join. Someone will be with you shortly; I can keep answering questions until then." zh "我已请团队成员加入，很快会有人来协助您；在此之前，我可以继续回答您的问题。" After hours: en "I've let the team know. We're closed now, so they'll reply from {next_open}. I can keep answering questions until then." zh "我已通知团队。我们现在休息，团队将于{next_open}起回复您；在此之前，我可以继续回答您的问题。" (`{next_open}` from `formatNextOpen`; no next opening in the horizon → en "when we're next open", zh "营业后"). The texts live in `@kaki/agent` `texts.ts` as `escalationNotice(lang, afterHours, nextOpen, now)`, since `inbox` can't import `agent`; callers pass it in as `noticeText`, so `requestEscalation(tx, { …, noticeText })` stays text-agnostic.
- `remindEscalation(tx, { tenantId, escalationId, now, hours })` → `'reminded' | 'resolved' | 'skipped'`: lock the escalation; resolved → `skipped`. Conversation has an owner or isn't `escalated` → resolve it (`claimed` or `closed`; the safety net if a path missed it) → `resolved`. Otherwise: the assignee not in `escalation_candidates()` → re-route (§5.2) and update `assignee_user_id`; `reminders + 1`; recipients = assignee, plus every owner and admin from reminder 3 on (D7); `notify(… kind: 'escalation_reminder', urgent: escalation.urgent, dedupeKey: 'escalation:{id}:r{n}')`; `next_reminder_at = addBusinessMinutes(now, reminderMinutes, hours)`; audit `escalation.reminded` `{ escalationId, n }` with the recipients' IDs. Reminders never post anything to the visitor.
- **Resolution inside `transition`**: when `from = 'escalated'` (to `human`, `ai` or `closed`), `transition` resolves the open escalation in the same transaction (`claimed` for `human` with `resolved_by` the actor, `closed`, `returned` for `ai`) and calls `cancelNotifications(tx, tenantId, { type: 'conversation', id })` for `escalation` and `escalation_reminder`. Every claim and close path therefore stops reminders and drops alerts not yet sent (and held digest items), without each caller remembering to.
- `escalationsOf(tx, tenantId, conversationIds)` → open escalation per conversation for summaries; `listConversations` assignee filter: `owner.user_id = $u or (no owner and open escalation assignee = $u)`; `none`: no owner and (no open escalation or a null assignee).
- `threadEvents` maps `escalation.assigned` to a divider (reason `escalated`, `subject` the assignee, `source`); `LIFECYCLE_ACTIONS` gains it. `escalation.reminded` is not a divider (noise).

### 5.2 Routing (`route(tx, tenantId)`, same file)

Reads `escalation_routing` `FOR UPDATE` (inserting the default row if none, `on conflict do nothing` then re-select, so concurrent first escalations serialise) and `escalation_candidates()`. `person` mode and the person is a candidate → the person. Otherwise round-robin: the first candidate whose `user_id` sorts after `last_assigned_user_id`, wrapping to the first; update the cursor. A `person` whose membership ended falls back to round-robin (D4) and logs `warn` with the tenant ID only. Unit-free: covered by integration (two tenants, three members, sequence A B C A; person mode; inactive person falls back; concurrent requests on two clients get different assignees).

### 5.3 Business minutes (`packages/tenancy/src/hours.ts`)

`addBusinessMinutes(hours, openOnPublicHolidays, from, minutes, exceptions?)` → `Date`: walks the same opening spans `openStatus` builds; from inside an opening it adds minutes, carrying any remainder into the next opening; from outside it starts at the next opening. `hours = null` → `from + minutes`. No opening within the horizon → `from + minutes` (never stall). Unit tests: inside an opening; crossing a close (remainder carried to the next morning); after hours (starts at opening); a public holiday skipped; open on a holiday by exception; midnight-spanning hours.

### 5.4 Agent (`packages/agent/src`)

- `dispatch.ts`: `ConversationPort` gains `escalate(source: 'visitor_asked' | 'unverified'): Promise<{ status: 'escalated' | 'already_escalated' | 'with_person' }>`. New `escalateHandler` (kind `escalate`, scopes `['visitor']`, args `z.object({}).passthrough()`, no consent, no boundary) registered in `visitorRegistry`; `run` calls `ctx.conversation.escalate('visitor_asked')` and returns `{ status }` with no `reply` key. Coaching scope refuses it (`scope`), as the dispatcher already does for kinds not in a registry's scope.
- `turn.ts`: when the plan's only non-safety task is `escalate` (no `answer` task), the turn stores no agent reply (the notice is the reply) and `store` records outcome `escalated` with `agent.decision` as usual; with answer tasks it answers them too. A compose outcome `escalated` (two failed verifications) calls `escalate('unverified')` after storing the escalation text. Both go through the port; the worker implements it with `requestEscalation` on the visitor zone in its own transaction (as `flag` does), passing the tenant hours from `gate.tenant`.
- `texts.ts`: `escalationNotice` (§5.1 step 4; used by the worker's port and the widget route). `unsure` gains the offer of a person (AGT-04): en "I'm not sure about that, so I won't guess. If you'd like, ask for a person and someone from the team will help." zh "这个问题我不太确定，所以不乱猜。如需要，您可以要求转人工，团队成员会来协助您。" The disclosure core gains design.md §6's "A person can take over at any time." / "您可以随时要求真人客服接手。" (FEAT-024 D2, now that takeover and this control exist).
- Fakes and evals: the in-memory port records `escalate` calls; `evals/cases/feat-044-escalation.json` adds dispatch-subset cases (EN, Singlish, 中文 "can I talk to a real person", "转人工", a multi-task "how much is the cake and can someone call me" → `answer` + `escalate`, and a non-request "is the person who bakes nice?" → no `escalate`).

## 6. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** (CHN-13 "talk to a person triggers escalation", UC-23 1) The widget's menu shows Talk to a person while the widget is live; pressing it moves the conversation to Escalated, shows the visitor "I've asked the team to join…" in their language within 2 s, and hides the item until staff join or leave; a second press can't create a second escalation.
- **AC-2** (AGT-06 `escalate_to_human` "tool calls are audited", UC-23 1) A visitor asking for a person in words (EN, Singlish, 中文) gets the same escalation through triage's `escalate` task and the dispatcher, audited `agent.dispatch` and `escalation.assigned` with IDs and codes only; answer tasks in the same message are still answered; the eval dispatch subset passes the new cases.
- **AC-3** (INB-05 "round-robin or a specific person", UC-23 2) In round-robin mode successive escalations go to each active member in turn, concurrent ones to different members; in person mode they go to that person; a person who is no longer an active member falls back to round-robin and the Team card says so.
- **AC-4** (INB-05 "notifies by email", LED-04, SRS §1.4) The assignee gets one escalation alert per escalation (FEAT-052 delivery and preferences): urgent during business hours for a visitor's request, not urgent for an unverified-reply escalation or after hours (D1); a repeated request sends no second alert.
- **AC-5** (INB-05 "reminders if not picked up within N minutes (default 10, configurable)… until claimed", UC-23 2b) With nobody taking over, a reminder goes out every N business minutes (default 10; 1–240 set on Team by owners and admins); from the third reminder owners and admins are reminded too; reminders stop, and unsent alerts are cancelled, as soon as someone takes over or the conversation leaves Escalated; a deactivated assignee's escalation is re-routed at the next reminder.
- **AC-6** (INB-05 "after hours, the visitor is told when staff will respond", AGT-09, UC-23 2a) After hours the notice gives the next opening time in SGT, the reminder clock starts at opening, and escalated conversations aren't auto-closed while unclaimed (D5).
- **AC-7** (INB-01 assignee, FEAT-043 D7) The inbox's assignee filter matches the owner, else the open escalation's assignee; escalated rows show the assignee and wait time; the conversation shows the escalation strip and a dated divider "Escalated to {name}".
- **AC-8** (AGT-04 offer of a person, AGT-11) The unsure reply offers a person, and the AI disclosure says a person can take over, in EN and 中文.
- **AC-9** (ADR-005, ADR-014, ADR-015) `escalations` and `escalation_routing` have forced RLS (tenant B sees nothing of A's); `escalation_candidates()` returns only the current tenant's active members' IDs; every new route is 404 or 403 across tenants and roles; audit payloads carry no names, emails or message text.
- **AC-10** (SCR-WGT-002, SCR-APP-014, SCR-APP-015, SCR-APP-045, NFR-09, NFR-19) The widget's menu item, asking and failed states and notice; the inbox's escalated rows and filter; the escalation strip and divider; and the Team card's states (read-only, saving, saved, errors, person inactive) render as in ui-design.md; at 375 px nothing scrolls sideways; axe reports no serious violations.

## 7. Decisions

- **D1 — Urgency (Ken, 2026-11-01: not urgent after hours).** A visitor's request (button or words) is urgent during business hours, so it bypasses quiet hours (SRS §1.4). After hours it is not urgent: the visitor has been told the return time (AGT-09), so nobody waits live, and the alert goes out normally or into the quiet-hours digest. An unverified-reply escalation is never urgent (not visitor-requested). Rejected (on the card): always urgent per §1.4's letter.
- **D2 — Routing is set on Team (SCR-APP-045), not a new screen.** Routing is about who on the team gets escalations; the Team page already lists the members it picks from, and owners and admins manage it (`team.manage`). The plan row names SCR-APP-014 and SCR-WGT-002 only; SCR-APP-045 (p) and SCR-APP-015 (p) are added here (§8). Rejected: Rules and approvals (approval SLA lives there, but rules are about what the AI may say) and a new SCR ID for one card.
- **D3 — Routing reads members through a SECURITY DEFINER function.** The visitor zone (agent-worker, widget API) can't read `memberships` (identity zone only). `escalation_candidates()` gives it the current tenant's active member IDs and roles and nothing else, the 0037 pattern. Rejected: routing in the identity zone with a second transaction (the escalation, its assignee and the alert must commit together).
- **D4 — Round-robin over all active members, ordered by user ID, with a cursor.** Every role holds `inbox.reply`. The cursor makes the order fair across restarts; `FOR UPDATE` on the routing row serialises concurrent escalations. A named person who left falls back to round-robin rather than to nobody. Rejected: least-loaded (needs presence, which doesn't exist) and skipping members who muted escalation email (muting is a channel preference, not availability).
- **D5 — Auto-close skips escalated conversations.** A visitor who asked for a person and went quiet must still be followed up; closing after 30 minutes would silently drop the request and stop reminders. Staff close it by hand (resolving the escalation) or take it over. FEAT-042's auto-close test gains this case; its other cases stand.
- **D6 — Reminders are their own notification kind, muted with escalations.** `escalation_reminder` gets its own email subject ("Reminder: a customer is still waiting for a person") and dedupe per reminder; the sender (`send.ts`, the mute check) maps it to the `escalation` event for the member's mute (no new preference row). The template change is in `templates.ts`; the link is the conversation.
- **D7 — From the third reminder, owners and admins are reminded too.** "Reminders repeat until claimed" must not depend on one person reading email; after 30 business minutes (default) the people who can reassign (FEAT-043 D1) hear about it. Earlier reminders go to the assignee only, so a team isn't flooded.
- **D8 — The button escalates without the model; words go through triage.** FEAT-033 D2 and ADR-025: a button press is a deterministic intent with its own endpoint; a typed request is interpreted by triage and acted on by the dispatcher's handler. Both end in `requestEscalation`, so routing, alerts and the notice are identical.
- **D9 — The AI keeps answering while escalated.** The gate is unchanged (ADR-013 d3 closes it only for an owner); the notice says so. A visitor waiting at 3am still gets KB answers (AGT-09).
